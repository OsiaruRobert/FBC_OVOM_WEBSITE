Why We Exist

Our Mission & Vision

Two convictions that shape everything we do as a church family.

Our Mission

To make disciples of Jesus Christ by preaching the gospel with clarity, teaching the whole counsel of scripture, and equipping every believer in Ovom and beyond to worship faithfully, grow in fellowship, and serve their community with love.

Our Vision

A church where every family in Yenagoa knows the love of Christ, generations worship together under one roof, and disciples are sent out — including through our five mission churches — to plant seeds of hope across Bayelsa State and beyond.

Our Story

Rooted in scripture. Built for Ovom.

First Baptist Church Ovom began as a small gathering of believers committed to prayer and the teaching of God's Word. Over the decades, that gathering has grown into a diverse congregation of families, students, and elders — united by one confession of faith and one calling to love our neighbours well.

We hold to the authority of scripture, the centrality of the gospel, and the priesthood of every believer — affiliated with the Bayelsa Baptist Conference and the Nigerian Baptist Convention. Whether you grew up in church or are walking through our doors for the first time, you'll find a seat saved for you.

Grow Where You're Planted

Church Organisations

From little children to elders, every member of FBC Ovom belongs to a fellowship built for their season of life.

Men's Ministry

MMU — Standing Firm, Leading Well

The men of FBC Ovom are called to lead their homes and community with integrity, faith and service. MMU brings men together across every stage of life — from boyhood to fatherhood — for discipleship, accountability and outreach. FBC Ovom is proud to host the MMU 46th National Congress, Tue 18th – Sat 22nd August 2026, right here in Bayelsa.

Adult Men · Mon (1st & 3rd), 4:30pm

Men's Missionary Union (MMU)

A fellowship for the men of the church built around prayer, biblical manhood, mentorship of younger men, and mission support for the wider church family and community outreach projects.

Young Boys

Royal Ambassadors (R.A)

A discipleship and character-building programme for young boys, teaching biblical values, leadership, teamwork and service through age-appropriate activities, badges and mission projects. Current focus: "Keys of the Kingdom" — Matthew 16:19.

Women's Ministry

WMU — Nurturing Faith Across Every Season

From little girls to married women, WMU weaves together fellowships that nurture, disciple and empower the women and girls of FBC Ovom at every stage of their walk with Christ.

Married Women · Mon, 4:30pm

Women's Missionary Union (WMU)

A fellowship of married women committed to prayer, Bible study, mentorship of younger women, and mission support — the backbone of hospitality and welfare ministry in the church.

Young Single Ladies · Mon (2nd & 4th), 4:30pm

Lydia Fellowship

Named after Lydia of Thyatira, this fellowship equips young single women to grow in purpose, purity and purposeful living while building lasting sisterhood. Lydia Sunday/Week runs 2nd–5th August.

Young Girls · Mon, 4:30pm

Girls' Auxiliary (G.A)

A mission-focused programme for young girls, teaching scripture, character and service through creative activities, badge-work and mentorship from older women of the church.

Little Children · Mon, 4:30pm

Sunbeam

Our youngest fellowship, introducing little children to the love of Jesus through songs, Bible stories and simple scripture memorisation in a joyful, nurturing setting.

Youth Ministry

Youths — Alive, Grounded, Sent

Our youth ministries create a home for teenagers and young adults navigating faith, identity and calling — both within the church and on campus.

Youths · Mon (1st & 3rd), 4:30pm

Youths Alive

A vibrant fellowship for youths and young adults built around worship, honest conversations about faith and life, mentorship, and community outreach.

Students · Fri, 5:00pm

Baptist Students Fellowship (BSF)

A campus-focused fellowship supporting students in tertiary institutions to stay rooted in faith through Bible study, prayer meetings and peer discipleship.

Next Generation

Teenagers & Children

Age-graded classes and activities that lay a lasting biblical foundation for our teenagers and children, taught by trained volunteers who genuinely love the next generation. A Holiday Bible School for children & teenagers runs each July.

Teenagers · Sun, 10:00am

Teen Church

A dedicated space for teenagers to wrestle with real questions of faith, build friendships, and grow in scripture through relatable, age-appropriate teaching.

Children · Sun, 10:00am

Children's Church

Safe, joyful Bible teaching for children through storytelling, music and creative learning, running alongside every Sunday service.
